# Engaging on other people's posts

Read [runtime](references/runtime.md), [privacy](references/privacy.md) and
[engagement](references/engagement.md). Use [evidence](references/evidence.md)
for why replies are a networking tool on X.

## Work from the real post

Ask for the post text or link if it's missing; don't invent praise or
context. Never invent handles. Build reply lists only from accounts the user
names or already follows.

## Draft

Give two or three reply options, best first. Each makes one move: add the
missing step, a real example, a respectful counterpoint, a sharper version,
or a genuine question. One to three sentences. Stands alone. No pitching,
no links to the user's things, no dunking or attacking creators, no filler.
Drafts are raw material: tell the user to rewrite the one they pick in their
own words before posting. Unedited AI replies read as slop and hurt the
account.

For a fast-rising post (a "viral moment" in the reference), reply early or,
if it's hours old and crowded, suggest a quote post that adds something
real. Skip sensitive topics no matter the reach.

## Deliver

Return the engagement output format from the reference, with a one-line
"why" for each option. Skip bait or political posts and say so. Use
`x-reply` for replies under the user's own posts.
