# Privacy, rights, and platform integrity

Access does not imply permission to publish. For client work, calls, DMs,
screenshots, or private context, establish what the user may reuse and who
else is identifiable. Removing a name alone may not prevent re-identification.
If consent is unclear, draft an abstract example labeled hypothetical, or ask.
Never turn a hypothetical into a personal testimony.

- No invented results, quotes, customers, testimonials, credentials, prices,
  dates, or scarcity. Use `{{RESULT}}` and leave it for the user to fill.
- Personal details only when the user supplies them for the post. Don't pull
  health, family, finances, or immigration details from other notes.
- Never invent X handles. Suggest reply targets only from accounts the user
  names or already follows.
- Keep filled context, exports, and voice samples outside the public project.
- Analyze mechanisms rather than copying other creators' posts. Steal formats,
  not content. Credit ideas you borrow.
- No automation that violates X's rules: no bots, no engagement pods, no
  purchased engagement, no scraping.
- Niche research is reading and summarizing patterns. Don't collect or store
  other people's personal data, and keep notes on other accounts to formats,
  hooks and gaps.
