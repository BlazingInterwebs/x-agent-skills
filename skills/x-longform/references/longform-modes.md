# Long post, thread, or Article

| Format | Use when | Structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Long post** (subscribers, up to 25,000 chars) | One story or breakdown, 6 to 15 lines | Hook line, then short paragraphs; the hook must work before "Show more" |
| **Thread** | Steps that each stand alone and could each get replies | Post 1 works alone; number the steps; last post closes the loop without a bait ask |
| **X Article** (Premium since Jan 7, 2026) | A deep piece tied to a story chapter or a long video; once a week or less | Clear-promise title, scannable sections, embedded posts or images where they help, cover image with one idea |

Threads vs long posts: X doesn't publish a comparison. INTERPRETATION: the
author-diversity decay and "each post uses some reach" both lean toward one
strong item over many weak ones. A long post keeps all engagement in one place.

Articles: Bier wrote in March 2026 that X "decided to reward Articles instead
of single posts" (context: a creator incentive). Treat that as a signal X
wants Articles, not a ranking rule. Ryan's "Distribution 101" is itself an
Article and names Articles as the X-native long format.

Titles: big promise, clear outcome, no confusion. Covers: their only job is
to get the click; less is more.
