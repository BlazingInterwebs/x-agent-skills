# X posts

Read [runtime](references/runtime.md) and [growth](references/growth.md). Use
[surfaces](references/surfaces.md) when choosing a format, [privacy](references/privacy.md)
when source material involves clients or private life, and
[post modes](references/post-modes.md) for hooks, mix and packaging.

## Develop the piece

Establish the audience, today's mix and funnel stage, and the actual facts.
If the user supplies a pattern brief, use its shapes, not anyone's wording.
Pick one idea. If
there are two ideas, that's two posts. Write three hooks of different types
and pick one. Default to a single post under five sentences. Move to
`x-longform` only if the idea genuinely needs more room.

Make the first line carry the post. Name one specific thing early so a
stranger can tell who it's for. A two-step post needs a real, cleared result;
without one, switch to a thought post. A personal post doesn't need a lesson.
A fast take needs the source checked and a real view added; if the news is
unconfirmed, say so or skip it. A how-to should be worth saving or sending.

## Self-check

One idea, stands alone, no invented proof, no bait ask, no politics,
ragebait or attacks on other creators, not generic AI slop, matches the
user's voice and punctuation rules, identity legible, the hook opens a gap
the post closes.
Any CTA is soft, one line, real, allowed by context, and only on a trust or
sales post. Links follow the user's link placement.

## Deliver

Return the draft in the runtime output format with one alternate hook.
State anything the user must confirm. The user posts it. Never schedule or
publish.

## Handoffs

Use `x-longform` for long posts, threads and Articles; `x-human` for voice
revision; `x-plan` for a week of slots; `x-audit` for results. Without a
sibling, give only the scoped draft and say what remains.
