# Audience growth without engagement bait

Audience growth is a first-class objective. It is not a guarantee, and it is
not a reason to make every post promotional. Nothing here promises reach; the
For You feed is personalized per viewer and X changes parameters often.

## Story first (from Ryan's "Distribution 101")

Content is a series of chapters that point at one end state.

1. **Macro picture.** What should people believe, how should they feel, which
   pain do you solve, why listen to you? Who are you, where did you start,
   what do you believe, who is it for, what's your edge, where are you going,
   what's the payoff?
2. **Reverse-engineer the chapters.** Start at the end state and work back to
   the beliefs each post should build.
3. **Voice.** Personality, what you'd never say, tone. Be yourself; don't
   hand the voice to a generic AI style.

Use the user's supplied story and pillars. If they're missing, mark them as
`{{PILLARS}}`, `{{OFFER}}`, `{{CTA}}` and don't guess.

## Content mix

A rotating mix keeps an account human and stops audience fatigue. A
working default (replace with the user's mix from context):

| Mix | What it is | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Thought** | A real question or observation the audience actually wrestles with | Real question, never engagement bait. The user must want the answers. |
| **Two-step** | A concrete, true result, then a quiet "who's next" | The result must be real and cleared. The ask is one short line, no hype. |
| **Personal** | Life, work, place, freedom | No bow-tied lesson. Let the moment be the post. |

Pitching stays rare and only with a real `{{CTA}}`.

## Growth loop on X

Real topic -> clear hook -> standalone payoff -> conversation (replies) ->
mutual relationships -> measured iteration.

Why this order, from X's open-source ranking code (see [evidence](evidence.md)):
replies and quotes carry weight 5.0, sends via DM 5.0, copy-link shares 20.0,
follows 4.0, likes 0.66. "Not interested," mutes, blocks and reports carry
large negative weights. Mutual follows get a reply boost (15.0, July 2026).

## Growth tips (Ryan, "Distribution 101")

- Build your network by actually engaging, especially on written platforms like X.
- Steal formats, not content, from adjacent niches.
- Double down on outliers: when something pops, make 5 more versions.
- Point every channel to another (X to newsletter, YouTube to X).
- Turn one idea into many through repurposing.
- Spend closer to 50/50 on packaging and content, not 95/5.

## Never

- Ask for likes, reposts, or follows. X's Original Content Rewards terms list
  "repeatedly instructing users to engage" as a violation.
- Ragebait, fake controversy, or manufacturing an opposing group for a hook.
- Strings of low-effort posts ("gm," three-word replies). X's head of product
  said each post spends some of the day's reach (Jan 2026).
- Invented results, testimonials, metrics, prices, or scarcity.
