# Profile and presence

The profile is the sales page. It turns a reply or a post into a follow,
and a follow into one clear next step (operator framing: Ryan, Oct 26, 2026,
UNVERIFIED as a rule). "Follow author" is a
modeled action with weight 4.0 in the ranking code, and a stranger decides
in a few seconds. The user makes all changes.

## Display name
Shows next to every reply, so it works as a label: name, optionally plus a
short role (`Name | Role`). Avoid emoji strings and hype tags. Changing the
display name on a verified account may trigger a checkmark re-review
(reported by users; UNVERIFIED current policy).

## Handle change checklist (UNVERIFIED details, confirm in X Help at the time)
- Old @mentions and links using the old handle may stop pointing to the
  user, and the old handle can be claimed by someone else.
- Update links on every other channel and website the same day.
- Change on a quiet day, then pin a short post that says who the user is.
- Never call a handle available without checking.

## Bio (160 characters)
Who you help + what changes for them + one human detail. No hype. Every claim
(years of experience, dates, results) must be something the user confirms.
Count characters after edits.

## Link
One link to the user's `{{CTA}}` destination. Until it exists, the user's
main channel or website.

## Header (1500 x 500)
One idea readable on a phone: a line in large type, or a real photo of the
user working. No collage, logo wall or fake stats. Keep key content central;
the avatar covers the lower left and the crop differs by device. An
optional short "who it's for" line can make the header do sales-page work.

## Photo
Clear, current face photo, the same as other channels.

## Pinned post
The post that best answers "why follow?" An intro long post (who, what they
post about, one line on `{{OFFER}}` with `{{CTA}}`), a best-performing post
refreshed monthly, or an Article laying out the story chapters.

## Onboarding library
Before chasing volume, have 5 to 10 evergreen pieces a new visitor can find
from the pinned post (a long post or Article that links or quotes them).
Operator advice (Hades, Aug 2026; UNVERIFIED). Refresh as winners appear.

## One next step
Bio, pinned post, header and link should all point to the same next step
(`{{CTA}}`): one offer, one destination, said the same way. If the user's
context rules out an offer for now, point everything at an allowed one.

## Checklist
- [ ] Name says who they are
- [ ] Bio says who they help and what changes, in plain words
- [ ] Header has one readable idea
- [ ] Link goes to one real destination
- [ ] Pinned post explains why to follow
- [ ] Bio, pinned post, header and link point to the same one next step
- [ ] A new visitor can reach 5 to 10 evergreen pieces from the pinned post
- [ ] Last 10 posts match the bio (no leftover off-topic or political posts)
