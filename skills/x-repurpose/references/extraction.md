# Repurposing: one idea, many shapes

- X is the test lab (Ryan). Winners on X become long-video topics, short
  video scripts, or sections of `{{OFFER}}` material.
- A long video becomes a single post with its sharpest line, a long post with
  the steps, and an X Article for the full version.
- An X Article becomes a short thread of its strongest points weeks later.
- Point every channel to another: X to `{{CTA}}` via bio and pinned post,
  video to X, X to the newsletter if one exists.
- Write each version natively. Don't paste other platforms' captions or
  descriptions onto X.
- Each extracted idea needs its own hook and payoff and must make sense to
  someone who never saw the source.
- One long piece can feed many short ones: for example about 10 posts, a few
  reply ideas and an Article from one long video (operator loop, Hades,
  Aug 2026; UNVERIFIED as a ratio).

## Existing assets into Articles

Operator advice (Chan, Oct 26, 2026; UNVERIFIED). Assets the user already has
often make the strongest X Articles:

| Asset | Article |
| --- | --- |
| A post that outperformed | The expanded version with the full depth |
| A lead magnet or checklist | A public version that shows the expertise |
| A client result | A case study from A to B, only with a cleared `{{RESULT}}` and the client's consent |
| An internal SOP or process | A process or authority piece |

Route each to `x-longform` with the asset, the reuse permission, and a first
idea for the packaging (banner, title, intro).

## Brief format
```
Source: <title, timestamp or section>
Idea: <one sentence>
Format: single | long post | thread | Article | quote of an existing Article
Hook type: <type>
Reuse permission: <confirmed | ask the user>
```
