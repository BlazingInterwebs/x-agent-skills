# Spaces and group chats

Read [runtime](references/runtime.md), [surfaces](references/surfaces.md),
[privacy](references/privacy.md) and [space modes](references/space-modes.md).

Check the current state first: Communities shut down on May 30, 2026; XChat
group chats with joinable links and Spaces remain. Treat Spaces as an
occasional test, not a core routine, unless the user's time allows.

Prepare a single practical topic, a co-host from the user's existing network,
a short outline, and a recap post for `x-post`. Never invite people or post
links on the user's behalf. Judge the test in `x-audit`.
