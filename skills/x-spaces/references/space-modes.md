# Spaces, group chats, and the end of Communities

## Communities: gone
X shut down Communities on May 30, 2026, citing low usage and spam
(TechCrunch, Apr 23, 2026). Advice to "post in Communities" is outdated. X
points to XChat group chats with joinable public links (Bier, Apr 22, 2026:
up to 350 members and growing) and custom timelines.

## Group chats
A small owned space for clients or a program later. Whether to open one is an
`{{OFFER}}` decision. Draft the description and rules only; the user creates it.

## Spaces
- Spaces still exist; hosting is from the mobile app (third-party help
  article, UNVERIFIED current details). X said little about Spaces in 2026.
- Low priority for a 30 to 45 minute hand-posting day. If tried: co-host with
  a peer from the reply list so there's an audience on day one, pick a single
  practical topic, keep it short, and post a recap afterward.
- Test once, then judge it in the weekly review.
